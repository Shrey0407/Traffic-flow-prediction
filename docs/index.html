Research question

Can a model trained on past speeds, weather, and calendar information predict the average speed on road segments two hours ahead, and how much does it beat simple baselines?

  • Row unit: one road sensor (segment) at one hour.
  • Target: average speed on that segment at hour t + 2, in km/h.
  • Prediction time: hour t, using only information available at t.

Data

PEMS-BAY, Caltrans PeMS via the DCRNN repository, 8 segments, hourly, Jan-Jun 2017. 0.023% of hours missing (one outage hour in March, same for all segments). No duplicates. Segment 400100 has a 24 h flat run.

Limitations

  • Results describe these segments and this period only.
  • Sensor outages affect both features and target.
  • No incident, roadworks, or event data.
  • Weather is observed at time t, not forecast.
  • Patterns shift across seasons and infrastructure changes.
  • Prediction is association, not causation.
